# MERCUR-REUSE-MATRIX.md
# Didar B2B Gold Platform — Native Mercur Reuse & Gap Matrix
# Date: October 2, 2026 | Status: Official Audit Artifact | Target Baseline: MercurJS (Medusa v2 Core)

This matrix defines the exact relationship between the native commerce capabilities of Mercur (MercurJS / Medusa v2 multi-vendor marketplace framework) and the specialized requirements of Didar (B2B Gold Jewelry & Bullion Value Chain Marketplace in Iran/Middle East).

---

## 1. Executive Architecture Summary

Mercur provides a robust, modular multi-vendor marketplace foundation based on Medusa v2 (Modules, Workflows, Links, Admin Extensions). However, standard ecommerce assumptions (fixed fiat currency unit pricing, single-step direct checkout, open vendor identity, unverified inventory counts, third-party payment gateways like Stripe) fundamentally conflict with the high-stakes physical and financial realities of the Iranian gold market:
1. **Commodity-Backed Dual Obligations:** Gold items cannot be sold for a static fiat sum; every transaction involves an 18K/750 gold weight obligation plus a fiat manufacturing wage (*ojrat*), margin, and Article 26 tax calculation.
2. **Blind Marketplace & Sourcing Intermediation:** Retailers must never see or contact workshop suppliers directly; Didar acts as the central counterparty, risk underwriter, assay verifier, and clearinghouse.
3. **Item-Level Physical Serialization (UIDs):** Gold items have metallurgical variance (net weight vs stone weight vs gross weight) and cannot be tracked by simple quantity counters; each piece requires an individual Unique Item Identifier (UID) minted post-intake.
4. **Field Agent & Bag Custody Operations:** Sales and replenishment occur via traveling field agents equipped with Sell-Bags, Sample-Bags, and armored logistics escorts.

The strategy is therefore **selective leverage**: reuse Mercur's core data models, multi-vendor seller identity, customer/auth primitives, and workflow orchestrators while extending them with gold pricing adapters, and building dedicated Didar modules for physical intake, dual ledgers, Zarrin ERP sync, agent bags, and AI sales intelligence.

## 3. Summary of Leverage Statistics

- **Native Mercur Reusable (with direct mapping):** 6 capabilities (~18%)
- **Native Mercur Extendable (adding Didar metadata & hooks):** 8 capabilities (~24%)
- **Native Mercur Replaceable (conflicts with gold market mechanics):** 6 capabilities (~18%)
- **Genuinely New Didar Modules Required:** 14 capabilities (~41%)

**Conclusion:**
Mercur provides a valuable ~40% accelerator for generic multi-vendor marketplace infrastructure (hierarchical categories, product/variant data models, multi-vendor seller identity, stock locations, fulfillment scaffolding, and event subscribers). However, approximately ~60% of the platform's core commercial value consists of specialized gold value chain mechanisms (commodity spot pricing, physical intake/assaying, UID serialization, dual subledgers, Zarrin ERP sync, agent bags, field operations, B2B enablement, and Maison luxury CMS) that must be implemented as dedicated Didar modules.

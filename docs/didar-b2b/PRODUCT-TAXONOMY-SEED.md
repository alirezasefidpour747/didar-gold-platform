# PRODUCT-TAXONOMY-SEED.md --- Didar Initial Gold Product Taxonomy

**Status:** Approved initial P01 taxonomy source\
**Date:** 2026-10-01

Rules:

-   Mapping: `Main Category → Product Category → Subcategory → Product`.
-   This table is the deterministic initial P01 taxonomy seed.
-   Use stable language-neutral identities/codes.
-   Persian and English labels are translations, not separate
    categories.
-   Seed must be idempotent.
-   Suppliers may select active nodes but may not mutate shared
    taxonomy.
-   Didar Product Ops / Super Admin govern taxonomy.
-   Used nodes are deactivated rather than physically deleted.
-   Storage is ready for `fa/en/ar/fr`; do not invent missing
    Arabic/French business translations.
